# 120-Second Pitch Script: HoneyBee for SMEs

This script is a practical walkthrough for a 2-minute screen recording, focusing on the core value propositions for judges.

---

## 0:00 - 0:15 | Intro: The Hive Ecosystem
**Action**: Start on the **Dashboard (The Hive)**. Hover over the health score and Honey Gems.
**Dialogue**: "Hi! We built HoneyBee—a strategic ecosystem for Malaysian SMEs. We noticed small business owners spend too much time on paperwork and not enough on strategy. HoneyBee turns their data into a 'Hive' of actionable growth insights."

## 0:15 - 0:50 | Feature 1: AI Vision Marketplace
**Action**: Go to **Marketplace**. Click 'List Product'. Upload/Drop a product image. Show the 'Analyzing Vision' animation. Watch as the Title, Description, and Price (RM) fill in automatically.
**Dialogue**: "Our 'Bee-Vision AI' is a game-changer. Watch this: I just upload a photo of my product, and the AI identifies the item, drafts a high-converting description, and suggests a fair RM price based on market trends. It turns a 10-minute chore into a 5-second breeze, allowing SMEs to scale their digital presence instantly."

## 0:50 - 1:25 | Feature 2: Finance & AI Strategy
**Action**: Click **Finance Suite**. Show the transaction ledger. Then click **AI SME Advisor**. (Demonstrate the paywall or the upgraded advisor view).
**Dialogue**: "Numbers without strategy are just noise. Our Finance Suite tracks every ringgit, which then flows into our AI SME Advisor. For our Pro members—the 'Queen Bees'—this advisor identifies financial risks and generates specific tasks, like 'Optimizing your SKU system' if stock-outs are predicted."

## 1:25 - 1:45 | Feature 3: Community & Academy
**Action**: Click **Community Hub**. Scroll through the social feed. Quickly click on **Micro-Learning**.
**Dialogue**: "Business is better together. Our Community Hub lets SMEs share success stories and collaborate on deals. And when they're ready to learn, our Academy offers bite-sized, RM-focused courses on leadership and digital marketing."

## 1:45 - 2:00 | Conclusion: The Buzz
**Action**: Back to **Dashboard**. Hover over the 'Honey Gems' rewards.
**Dialogue**: "Every action earns 'Honey Gems' for expert mentorship or marketplace perks. HoneyBee isn't just a management tool; it's a growth engine for the next generation of Malaysian entrepreneurs. Let's make your business buzz. Thank you!"

---

## Technical Performance Check (For Judges)
- **Framework**: React 18 + Vite + Tailwind CSS.
- **Intelligence**: Gemini 1.5 Pro powers the Vision Analysis and the SME Strategic Advisor.
- **SaaS Model**: Built-in subscription tiers (Worker Bee vs. Drone Bee) with functional payment gateways.
